# USPSTF — Lung Cancer: Screening (2021) — trechos literais

Fonte: https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/lung-cancer-screening · Acesso: 15/09/2026 · Grau B

- População: "Adults aged 50 to 80 years"
- Tabagismo: "20 pack-year smoking history" (mínimo)
- Status: "currently smoke or have quit within the past 15 years"
- Periodicidade: "annual screening" with low-dose computed tomography (LDCT)
- Interromper: "once a person has not smoked for 15 years" OU "develops a health problem that substantially limits life expectancy or the ability or willingness to have curative lung surgery"

Usado em: C-006.
